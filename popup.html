Open the console

  1. Open Developer Tools with F12 or Ctrl + Shift + I.
  2. Select the Better Console tab in DevTools.
  3. Refresh the webpage to capture its console output from the start.

The console lives in DevTools; the toolbar popup is this quick-start panel.
